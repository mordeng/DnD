### **Alchemical Homunculus (Blobulus) (CR 8)**
![[blobulus.webp]]
- **Medium Construct**
- **Armor Class**: 18 (natural armor, alchemical plating)
- **Hit Points**: 136 (16d8 + 64)
- **Speed**: 30 ft, Fly 40 ft.

---

**STR**: 16 (+3)  
**DEX**: 18 (+4)  
**CON**: 18 (+4)  
**INT**: 12 (+1)  
**WIS**: 15 (+2)  
**CHA**: 8 (-1)

---

**Saving Throws**: DEX +8, CON +8  
**Skills**: Perception +6, Stealth +8  
**Damage Resistances**: Cold, Lightning, Acid, bluedodging  
**Damage Immunities**: Poison, Psychic  
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Poisoned, Frightened, Exhaustion  
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16  
**Languages**: Understands Common and Alchemical but can’t speak

---

### **Traits:**

1. **Alchemical Regeneration**:  
    At the start of its turn, the Homunculus regains 10 hit points if it has at least 1 hit point remaining.
    
2. **Arcane Absorption**:  
    Whenever the Homunculus is targeted by a spell, it can use its reaction to absorb the magic into its body, nullifying the spell’s effects on it. The homunculus stores the energy, gaining a bonus to its damage on its next turn equal to the spell’s level (1d6 per spell level).
    
4. **Magical Bond**:  
    The Homunculus shares a telepathic link with its artificer creator, and while within 100 feet of the artificer, it gains a +2 bonus to AC and attack rolls.

### **Actions:**

1. **Multiattack**:  
    The Homunculus makes two alchemical blade or alchemical bolt attacks.
    
2. **Alchemical Blade**:  
    _Melee  Attack_: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
    _Hit_: 15 (2d10 + 4) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) acid damage.
    
3. **Alchemical Bolt**:  
    _Ranged  Attack_: +8 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target.  
    _Hit_: 16 (2d8 + 4) force damage.
    
4. Alchemic Barf: Dex Saving Throw 16
	Splash 30 feet cone 6x6 / half
	If hit make 2d8 + 4 Acid Damage and throw a D20 add a Random for each of table 2 effects
	Take half dmg and no effect if succesfull.

---

### **Reactions:**

1. **Alchemical Shield**:  
    When a creature within 5 feet of the Homunculus is targeted by an attack, the Homunculus can use its reaction to give that creature +3 AC for the attack, potentially causing the attack to miss.

### **Tactics and Synergy with the Artificer:**

The Homunculus is designed to fight alongside a level 8 Artificer, whose abilities can further enhance the construct's effectiveness:

- The Artificer can use spells like _Shield_, _Cure Wounds_, and _Haste_ to support and amplify the Homunculus's powers.
- The Homunculus’s **Arcane Absorption** pairs well with the Artificer's spellcasting, allowing for extra damage output and a spell-nullifying defense.
- Together, their synergy boosts battlefield control, with the Homunculus acting as a heavy hitter and defender, while the Artificer offers ranged support, healing, and utility.
