# **🔥 Avernus Travel Mechanics & Events**

## **🚗 Vehicle-Based Mechanics**

### **Soul Fuel Dynamics**
- **Fuel Anxiety**: Each day of travel requires soul coins. Low fuel = desperate choices
- **Screaming Engines**: Soul coins literally scream when used as fuel - Wisdom saves for good characters
- **Coin Companions**: NPCs trapped in coins offer information/guidance in exchange for not being burned
- **Fuel Alternatives**: PCs can sacrifice HP to power machines (1d10 damage = 24h fuel, heals 1/day)

### **War Machine Events**
- **Breakdown Roulette**: Roll d20 each travel day. 1-2 = mechanical failure requiring repairs/parts
- **Hijacker Swarms**: Flying demons attempt to board moving vehicles
- **Infernal Traffic Jams**: Convoys of other war machines creating bottlenecks and territorial disputes

## **🌋 Environmental Hazards**

### **Weather Events** 
- **Soul Sandstorms**: Visibility drops to 10ft, screaming winds deal psychic damage
- **Rust Rain**: Corrodes weapons/armor, advantage on Survival to find shelter
- **Blood Tempests**: Red lightning, disadvantage on navigation

### **Terrain Dangers**
- **Bone Fields**: Roads made of fallen army remains - creaking sounds unnerve steeds/drivers
- **Hellhole Chasms**: Sudden abyssal rifts requiring driving/piloting checks to avoid
- **Styx Overflow**: Memory-erasing river floods roads - Constitution saves or lose memories

## **👹 Encounters & Social Events**

### **The Tarrasque Proximity**
- **Distant Rumbling**: Ominous tremors indicate the beast is stirring nearby
- **Evacuation Chaos**: Other travelers fleeing creates opportunities for ambush/aid
- **Tarrasque Tourism**: Foolhardy devils offering "safe" viewing spots (spoiler: they're not safe)

### **Political Intrigue**
- **Zariel's Patrols**: Loyalty tests, recruitment attempts, or hunting deserters
- **Gargauth Whispers**: Mirrors/reflective surfaces show tempting visions for susceptible characters
- **Demon Raids**: Chaotic attacks disrupting the established infernal order

## **🎭 Character-Specific Events**

### **For Kalamaris** (based on your character development notes)
- **Mirror Encounters**: Reflective surfaces show Gargauth's true form instead of Kalamaris
- **Devil Recognition**: NPCs address him as "Vessel" or "Carrier" - unsettling implications
- **Power Corruption**: Each use of demonic abilities makes Gargauth's influence stronger

## **🎲 Travel Encounter Table** (Roll d12 daily)

1. **Fuel Shortage**: Meet desperate travelers willing to trade/fight for soul coins
2. **Wandering Emporium**: Mobile marketplace appears with rare goods and information
3. **Bone Road Collapse**: Navigate around/through skeleton-built highway damage  
4. **Soul Coin Madness**: A coin's trapped soul provides cryptic warnings about upcoming danger
5. **Infernal Bureaucrats**: Devils demanding travel permits and bribes
6. **Styx Tributary**: Memory river blocks path - find ford or lose memories crossing
7. **Scrap Vultures**: Carrion creatures following the party, hoping for vehicle breakdown
8. **War Machine Graveyard**: Abandoned vehicles with potentially useful parts/fuel
9. **Zariel's Broadcast**: Magical propaganda affecting party morale/loyalty
10. **Refugee Convoy**: Fleeing mortals with information about dangers ahead
11. **Territorial Warlords**: Local devil princes demanding tribute for safe passage  
12. **Gargauth Vision**: Tempting illusions offering shortcuts that lead to traps

## **💰 Earning Infernal Currency**

### **Why Gold Doesn't Work**
- Most Avernian vendors refuse mortal gold ("What am I supposed to do with this shiny metal?")
- Devils mock gold as "worthless mortal trinkets"  
- Only a few desperate refugees or fellow mortals accept gold (at terrible exchange rates)

### **🛠️ Work Opportunities** (Roll d8)
1. **Convoy Guard Duty** - 5-15 Obsidian Chits per day, high risk
2. **War Machine Repairs** - 2-8 Chits per successful repair check
3. **Soul Coin Authentication** - 1 Chit per coin verified (requires Investigation/Arcana)
4. **Message Running** - 3-10 Chits for delivering between settlements
5. **Scrap Metal Collection** - 1 Chit per 50 lbs of usable metal found
6. **Demon Extermination** - 10-25 Chits per demon head delivered
7. **Map Making** - 20-50 Chits for accurate regional maps
8. **Translation Services** - 5-15 Chits (knowing Infernal/Abyssal is valuable)

### **🏆 Bounty & Reward Events**
- **Wanted Posters**: Devils post bounties for escaped souls, deserters (25-100 Chits)
- **Rescue Missions**: Save trapped merchants/soldiers (10-50 Chits reward)
- **Information Brokers**: Sell valuable intel about demon movements, safe routes
- **Gladiator Pits**: Win fights for prize money (high risk, high reward)

## **⚔️ THE SCREAMING CRATER - Warzone Scavenging**

*A massive battlefield crater where a recent demon/devil clash occurred*

### **Location Description**
- 2-mile wide crater filled with destroyed war machines, weapons, bodies
- Constant low moaning from trapped souls in damaged vehicles
- Scavenging crews work the edges, avoiding the dangerous center
- Occasional flare-ups as damaged machines explode

### **🎲 Scavenging Table** (Roll d12 per hour of searching)

| d12 | Find | Value |
|-----|------|-------|
| 1 | **Trap/Danger** - Unstable wreckage, live ordnance |  |
| 2-3 | **Nothing Useful** - Twisted metal, ash | - |
| 4-5 | **Scrap Metal** - 20-50 lbs | 1 Obsidian Chit |
| 6-7 | **Damaged Weapon** - Needs repair | 2-5 Chits (when fixed) |
| 8-9 | **Vehicle Parts** - Engine components, wheels | 5-15 Chits |
| 10 | **Intact Weapon/Armor** - Battle-ready | 10-25 Chits |
| 11 | **Soul Coin** - Hidden in wreckage | 1 Soul Coin |
| 12 | **Rare Find** - Magical item, intact war machine | 50+ Chits or special |

### **Crater Hazards** (Check each hour)
- **Unstable Ground**: Dexterity saves or fall into wreckage pits
- **Toxic Fumes**: Constitution saves vs poisoned condition  
- **Scavenger Gangs**: Other groups competing for salvage
- **Wounded Survivors**: Devils/demons still alive and hostile
- **Soul Echoes**: Psychic damage from traumatic battle memories

### **Special Locations in the Crater**
- **The Command Bunker**: Ruined devil fortress with intact vault (major treasure)
- **Demon Spawn Pool**: Corrupted area spawning minor demons
- **The Screaming Engine**: Massive war machine with dozens of soul coins trapped inside
- **Memory Fields**: Areas where Styx water pooled, causing reality distortions

## **🗺️ When to Use These Events**

### **Between Maps** (Overland Travel)
- **Daily travel mechanics** - fuel consumption, weather, navigation
- **Random encounter rolls** - use the d12 table above
- **Resource management** - soul coins, repairs, supplies
- **Long-distance threats** - Tarrasque proximity, major storms

### **On Maps** (Specific Locations)
- **Location-specific events** - Fort Knucklebone politics, Wandering Emporium trades
- **Character moments** - Kalamaris mirror encounters in reflective locations
- **Social encounters** - meeting specific NPCs, faction interactions
- **Set-piece encounters** - planned story beats, major battles

### **Best of Both Worlds**
- **Transition events** - things that happen as you arrive/leave locations
- **Cliffhangers** - end between-map travel with "you see smoke on the horizon" then resolve on the map
- **Consequence chains** - between-map events create situations that play out on maps

**Example Flow:**
1. Leave Fort Knucklebone
2. *Between maps*: Soul sandstorm forces detour, find refugee convoy
3. Arrive at Bronze Citadel outskirts
4. *On map*: Refugees turn out to be Zariel's spies, creating tension at the location
