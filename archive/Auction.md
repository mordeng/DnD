## **The Grand Auction of Baldur's Gate**

### **Setting and Atmosphere**

**Venue**: The auction takes place at **The Gilded Chalice**, an opulent grand hall located in the upscale district of Baldur's Gate. The venue is renowned for hosting exclusive events for the city's elite and influential figures.

**Exterior**:

- **Architecture**: A magnificent building adorned with marble columns, intricate carvings, and golden accents.
- **Security Presence**: Elite guards are stationed at the entrances, with arcane sentinels subtly patrolling the perimeter.
### **Arrival and Reception**

**Guest Entry**:

- **Invitation Verification**: Attendees present their gilded invitations at the entrance. Magical seals are inspected by arcane sentinels to prevent forgeries.
- **Security Screening**: Guests undergo discreet security checks for prohibited items. Weapons are peace-bonded or stored securely.
- **Welcoming Committee**: Elegant hosts greet guests, providing programs detailing the auction items and schedule.

**Networking Opportunity**:

- **Pre-Auction Mingling**: Before the auction begins, guests are encouraged to socialize in the adjacent **Silver Lounge**.
- **Interactions**: The party can engage with other attendees, gather information, and observe the competition.
- **Notable Conversations**:
  - **Madame Seraphine Redwyne** subtly probing others for their interests.
  - **Archmage Selene Windwhisper** discussing arcane theories with peers.
  - **Captain Helena Stormblade** observing the room with a tactical eye.

### **The Auction Begins**

**Auctioneer Introduction**:

- **Auctioneer**: **Vargas Blackwood**, a charismatic and experienced auctioneer known for his flair and professionalism.
- **Opening Remarks**: Vargas welcomes the esteemed guests, emphasizing the exclusivity and significance of the items up for bid.

**Presentation Format**:

- **Item Display**: Each item is brought onto the stage under a protective glass dome, sometimes enhanced with magical effects to highlight its features.
- **Description**: Vargas provides a detailed explanation of the item's properties, history, and potential uses.
- **Bidding Process**: Bids are placed using numbered paddles or discreet signals for privacy.

## **Auction Items and Events**

### **Item 1: Elixir of Ever-Waking**

**Description**:

- **Appearance**: A pale blue potion in a delicate crystal vial.
- **Effect**: Eliminates the need for sleep for 24 hours.

**Auction**:

- **Starting Bid**: Moderate price, attracting interest from scholars and merchants.
- **Bidding Parties**: The Arcane Consortium, House Drovos, and independent adventurers show interest.

**Event Between Items**:

- **Rumor Circulation**: Whisper, from the Shadow Hand Syndicate, spreads rumors about the side effects of the elixir to dissuade bidders.
- **Opportunity for the Party**: The players overhear conflicting information and can choose to investigate or use it to their advantage.

### **Item 2: Stoneskin Salve**

**Description**:

- **Appearance**: A thick, earthy ointment in a small metal tin.
- **Effect**: Grants resistance to physical damage by hardening the skin.

**Auction**:

- **Starting Bid**: Slightly higher due to its combat applications.
- **Bidding Parties**: The Iron Circle Mercenaries and House Drovos are primary bidders.

**Event Between Items**:

- **Minor Disruption**: A loud crash is heard backstage. Guards quickly investigate, and the auction pauses briefly.
- **Outcome**: It's discovered that a servant accidentally dropped a tray. Tension rises as guests become more alert.
- **Opportunity for the Party**: The distraction allows the party to slip away briefly to explore restricted areas or eavesdrop on private conversations.

### **Item 3: Liquid Sunlight**

**Description**:

- **Appearance**: A vial containing a radiant, glowing liquid.
- **Effect**: Emits daylight when uncorked.

**Auction**:

- **Starting Bid**: High interest due to its utility against creatures of darkness.
- **Bidding Parties**: The Azure Enclave and the Arcane Consortium compete intensely.

**Event Between Items**:

- **Secret Message**: A server discreetly hands the party a folded note, indicating someone wishes to meet them in the Silver Lounge.
- **Opportunity for the Party**: The party can choose to meet the mysterious individual, potentially leading to valuable information or a side quest.

### **Item 4: Binding Breeze Bottles**

**Description**:

- **Appearance**: Elegant glass bottles etched with swirling patterns.

**Auction**:

- **Starting Bid**: Moderate; attracts interest from law enforcement and tacticians.

**Event Between Items**:

- **Tension Rising**: A heated exchange occurs between Master Alchemist Seraphina Flintlock and Captain Helena Stormblade over previous bids.
- **Outcome**: Guards intervene to de-escalate the situation.
- **Opportunity for the Party**: They can intervene diplomatically, gaining favor with one or both parties.

### **Item 5: Volatile Growth Potion**

- **Starting Bid**: Very high due to its powerful effects.

### **Item 9: Potion of Elemental Transmutation**

**Description**:

- **Appearance**: A swirling concoction that changes color and texture.
- **Effect**: Transforms the drinker's body into elemental energy.

**Auction**:

- **Starting Bid**: Extremely high; attracts significant attention.
- **Bidding Parties**: The Azure Enclave, the Arcane Consortium, and the Veiled Dawn Cult are major contenders.

**Event Between Items**:

- **Magical Anomaly**: The potion reacts unpredictably, causing minor elemental effects in the hall (e.g., brief gusts of wind, sparks).
- **Outcome**: Arcane Sentinels stabilize the potion; some guests become uneasy.
- **Opportunity for the Party**: They might notice inconsistencies suggesting tampering, leading to further investigation.

### **Item 10: Unstable Alchemical Nexus**

**Description**:

- **Appearance**: An intricate apparatus with swirling energies contained within glass and metal components.
- **Effect**: Designed to tap into the raw forces of creation.

**Auction**:

- **Starting Bid**: Astronomical; this is the centerpiece of the auction.
- **Bidding Parties**: The RR Organization, the Arcane Consortium, and the Crimson Dawn Cult enter a fierce bidding war.

**Event During Auction**:

- **Climactic Disruption**:

  - **Sabotage Attempt**: The Shadow Hand Syndicate makes a bold move to steal the Nexus amidst the chaos.
  - **Chaos Ensues**: The Nexus becomes unstable, emitting flashes of light and bursts of energy.
  - **Security Response**: Guards scramble to contain the situation; some guests panic.
  - **Opportunity for the Party**:
    - **Heroic Intervention**: Assist in stabilizing the Nexus, preventing a potential disaster.
    - **Pursuit**: Chase after fleeing Syndicate members for more information or to retrieve stolen items.
    - **Undercover Actions**: Use the chaos to their advantage to acquire the Prismatic Alchemical Core or gather intelligence.

### **Special Item: Prismatic Alchemical Core (Lot 11)**

**Description**:

- **Appearance**: A multifaceted gemstone emitting a soft, iridescent glow.
- **Effect**: Catalyst for producing experimental elixirs with Blobulus.

**Auction**:

- **Starting Bid**: High due to its enigmatic allure.
- **Bidding Parties**: The RR Organization, House Drovos, and the Clockwork Collective show interest.

**Event During Auction**:

- **Blobby's Revelation**:

  - **Blobby Emerges**: Unable to contain its urgency, Blobulus appears openly, causing a stir among the attendees.
  - **Recognition**: Some parties realize the connection between Blobulus and the core.
  - **RR's Reaction**: The RR Organization, recognizing the significance, attempts to capture Blobulus.
  - **Opportunity for the Party**:
    - **Defend Blobby**: Protect Blobulus from capture, possibly engaging in combat with RR agents.
    - **Acquire the Core**: Use the distraction to secure the core, either through bidding or stealth.
    - **Escape Plan**: Plan a quick exit from the venue with Blobulus and the core.

## **Design of Guards and Bodyguards**

### **Auction Guards**

**Elite Auction Guards**:

- **Uniform**: Dark attire with gold trim, discreet yet authoritative.
- **Armaments**: Short swords, crossbows, and communication devices (e.g., sending stones).
- **Abilities**: High perception, trained in non-lethal takedowns to minimize collateral damage.

**Arcane Sentinels**:

- **Appearance**: Robes with arcane sigils, eyes glowing faintly when detecting magic.
- **Abilities**: **Detect Magic**, **Counterspell**, **Dispel Magic**, **Arcane Lock**.

**Construct Guardians**:

- **Design**: Metallic humanoid figures with smooth surfaces and minimalistic features.
- **Abilities**: **Immunity** to certain spells, **Strength** for restraining intruders, programmed to protect key areas.

### **Bodyguards**

**RR Organization's Crimson Blades**:

- **Uniform**: Sleek armor with crimson accents, concealed weapons.
- **Abilities**: **Multiattack**, **Evasion**, **Protection** fighting style.

**Iron Circle Soldiers**:

- **Appearance**: Heavy armor, shields with the Iron Circle emblem.
- **Abilities**: **Formation Tactics** (bonuses when fighting together), **Indomitable**.

**Arcane Wardens (Arcane Consortium)**:

- **Appearance**: Mage armor with defensive enchantments.
- **Abilities**: **Shield**, **Mage Armor**, **Blade Ward**, cantrips for self-defense.

**Shadow Enforcers (Shadow Hand Syndicate)**:

- **Appearance**: Dark clothing, masks covering their faces.
- **Abilities**: **Stealth Expertise**, **Sneak Attack**, **Poisoned Weapons**.

## **Suggestions for the Dungeon Master**

### **Pacing the Auction**

- **Adjust Timing**: Depending on player engagement, you can speed up or slow down the auction.
- **Player Involvement**: Encourage the party to bid on items, interact with NPCs, or react to events.

### **Managing Events**

- **Flexibility**: Be prepared to improvise if players take unexpected actions.
- **Consequences**: Ensure that the party's choices have meaningful outcomes, influencing future interactions.

### **Creating Tension**

- **Atmosphere**: Use descriptions to heighten senses—sounds of murmuring crowds, the weight of gazes, the glint of valuables.
- **Suspicion**: Emphasize the underlying tension among parties, hinting at ulterior motives.

### **Utilizing Blobulus**

- **Communication**: Since Blobulus can't speak, use expressive actions to convey urgency.
- **Plot Device**: Blobulus can lead the party to critical information or locations.
