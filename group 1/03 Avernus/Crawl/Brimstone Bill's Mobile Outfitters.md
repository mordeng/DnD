- **Quirk**: Always smoking a pipe that never burns out, claims it's "authentic Avernus tobacco"

---

## **💰 Currency System Explained**

### **Infernal Currency Hierarchy**
- **Soul Coins** (50 platinum value) - Rare, used as backing/major purchases
- **Spent Soul Coins** (1 platinum value) - Burned out coins, more common
- **Obsidian Chits** (1 gold value) - Common currency issued by warlords

### **What Bill Accepts**
- All infernal currencies above
- Material goods (weapons, armor, rare metals) 
- Information/favors
- **NO GOLD** - "What am I supposed to do with shiny mortal trinkets?"

### **What are Obsidian Chits?**
Small black volcanic glass tokens issued by Avernian warlords (Mad Maggie, Wandering Emporium, etc.) and backed by their soul coin stockpiles. Think "paper money" backed by gold - about 1,000 chits can be issued per soul coin held.

## **🤝 ITEMS BILL WANTS TO BUY**

### **High Value** (Will pay premium)
- **Fresh Soul Coins** - Pays 40 Obsidian Chits each
- **Rare Metals** (mithril, adamantine) - Varies by quality
- **Infernal Machine Parts** - 10-50 Obsidian Chits depending on rarity
- **Undamaged Weapons from Upper Planes** - 2-3x normal value

### **Medium Value** (Fair prices)
- **Quality Weapons/Armor** - Standard rates minus 20%
- **Gemstones** - Will appraise and pay 80% value
- **Spell Scrolls** - Particularly divination and protection magic
- **Information** - Maps, safe routes, faction movements (5-25 Obsidian Chits)

### **Low Value** (Will take if desperate)
- **Standard Equipment** - 50% of normal value
- **Damaged Goods** - Will repair and resell
- **Rations/Supplies** - Always needs basics for other customers

---

## **💡 Special Services**

- **Vehicle Repairs** - 10 Obsidian Chits per hull point restored
- **Map Updates** - 5 Obsidian Chits for current danger reports
- **Message Delivery** - Will carry letters to major settlements for 1 Obsidian Chit
- **Rumor Mill** - Free gossip with any purchase over 20 Obsidian Chits

## **🎲 Random Shop Events** (Roll d6)

1. **Surprise Inspection** - Devils demand to check Bill's "trading licenses"
2. **Competitor Arrival** - Another merchant shows up, starting a price war
3. **Desperate Customer** - Someone offers a soul coin for basic supplies
4. **Hijack Attempt** - Demons try to raid the shop during visit
5. **Bulk Discount** - Bill offers 20% off everything - he needs to move inventory fast
6. **Special Order** - Bill can get something specific in 1d4 days for double price
