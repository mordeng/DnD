Guild Artisan - Baldur’s Gate

Feature: Guild Membership

As an established and respected member of a guild, you can rely on certain benefits that membership provides. Your fellow guild members will provide you with lodging and food if necessary, and pay for your funeral if needed. In some cities and towns, a guildhall offers a central place to meet other members of your profession, which can be a good place to meet potential patrons, allies, or hirelings.

Guilds often wield tremendous political power. If you are accused of a crime, your guild will support you if a good case can be made for your innocence or the crime is justifiable. You can also gain access to powerful political figures through the guild, if you are a member in good standing. Such connections might require the donation of money or magic items to the guild’s coffers.

You must pay dues of 5 gp per month to the guild. If you miss payments, you must make up back dues to remain in the guild’s good graces.

**Baldur’s Gate Feature: Professional Courtesy**

You’re familiar with the city’s crews, their territories, and inter-crew politics. Choose one of the three districts of Baldur’s Gate: the Upper City, the Lower City, or the Outer City. This is the district where you conduct most of your business. Whenever you need information about something in one of that district’s neighborhoods, you can seek out crew members in that area and learn the local gossip. You can also gain unimpeded entry to nearly any bank, guild hall, place of business, workhouse, or crew meeting place in your district.

Backstory

Milo Quickspark's journey through life was marked by an insatiable curiosity, a passion for alchemy, and a culinary adventure that was equal parts delicious and disastrous. Born in the heart of a bustling gnome village within the mystical forests of Eldertree Glade, Milo's destiny seemed intertwined with the secrets of nature and the thrill of experimentation. From an early age, Milo displayed a knack for tinkering and a fascination with the hidden wonders of magic. In the kitchen, their culinary experiments were a source of both delight and concern for their family and friends. While they possessed a genuine love for cooking, their culinary creations often ranged from unconventional to utterly bizarre. Nevertheless, Milo's enthusiasm remained undiminished, and they approached cooking with the same zest as their alchemical pursuits. As Milo's alchemical talents grew, so did their culinary escapades. They ventured beyond traditional recipes, infusing their dishes with experimental ingredients and magical spices. The results were unpredictable, to say the least, with some meals becoming memorable feasts while others led to animated discussions on the merits of "adventurous" cuisine. Milo's culinary adventures became a source of humor and entertainment in their gnome village, with neighbors eagerly awaiting each new dish, never quite sure what to expect. Despite their culinary mishaps, Milo's endearing personality and genuine love for food endeared them to the community. One fateful day, Eldra Brightflask, a master alchemist, recognized Milo's potential and invited them to apprentice. Under Eldra's guidance, Milo honed their alchemical skills and developed a deeper appreciation for the art and science of potion-making. It was during this time that Milo's cautious and reflective nature began to emerge, tempered by their mentor's wisdom. When Milo decided to leave the village in search of new horizons, they carried with them not only their alchemical knowledge but also a set of well-used cook utensils. Their culinary experiments had left a trail of both laughter and awe, and they couldn't bear to part with the joy of cooking. Milo's adventures took them to the bustling streets of Baldur's Gate, a city known for its diverse cultures and culinary delights. Here, they continued their culinary escapades, experimenting with local ingredients and infusing their alchemical flair into every dish. While they may not have become a culinary master, their enthusiasm and unique approach to cooking earned them a reputation as the city's quirkiest and most adventurous chef. In addition to their love for cooking, Milo's past as a Guild Artisan in Baldur's Gate gave them a strong sense of community and a network of contacts within the city's artisans' guild. This background allowed them to navigate the city's vibrant marketplace, forging connections and acquiring rare alchemical ingredients. Now, Milo Quickspark roams the realms as a Level 5 Alchemist and culinary enthusiast, seeking new alchemical challenges and cooking up dishes that range from eccentric to extraordinary. They are a figure of both caution and ambition, ready to unlock the alchemical wonders that the world has to offer and serve up culinary surprises to anyone willing to take a bite. As Milo's journey unfolds, their unique blend of alchemical expertise and culinary creativity will continue to shape their destiny, leaving a trail of both magical elixirs and unforgettable flavors in their wake.
