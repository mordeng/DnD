# Volume 1: "The Gnome Who Knew Too Little"

**Target**: ~75,000 words | 30 chapters | ~2,500 words/chapter

---

## Premise

Six strangers survive a shipwreck on a mysterious island where the locals are losing their minds. The gnome alchemist narrating this story would very much like to solve the mystery scientifically, except his companions include a dramatic bird-pirate, a silent druid, a soldier hiding water powers, a grieving pirate with a boar, and a dwarf who cannot be wrong. Together they defeat a shadow dragon, cross frozen mountains, descend into a dwarven kingdom threatened by a magma elemental, and discover that the most dangerous thing in the world is caring about people.

## Key Adaptation Change

**Milo stays with the group throughout Volume 1.** In the campaign, Milo left early to join the RR-Guild. In the novel, he's present for everything -- the omniscient narrator frequently orbits him as the emotional center. His kidnapping happens at the END of Volume 1 as the cliffhanger.

---

## 5-Act Structure

### Act 1: The Island of Shifting Shadows (Chapters 1-6, ~15,000 words)
*Setup: 20% of volume*

**Goal**: Establish all six characters, their dynamics, and the mystery of Moreva.

| Ch | Title (Working) | Content | Humor/Emotion |
|----|----------------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | *In Which We Begin at the Bottom* | Milo wakes on a beach. Narrator introduces him, Blobby, the shipwreck. Meets the others one by one. Kalmaris is theatrical, Serox is already complaining. | Narrator voice established. Cooking gag setup. |
| 2 | *Regarding the Inadvisability of Tropical Cuisine* | Jungle survival. Milo cooks. The party develops evasion tactics. Longjohn/Blacky introduction. Lamil speaks to the forest. | First cooking disaster. Longjohn-Blacky bond. |
| 3 | *A Scientific Approach to Mass Hysteria* | Village discovered. Locals are acting wrong. Milo begins documenting symptoms. First subtle shadow-wrongness. | Adams-style: Milo treats madness as a research paper. |
| 4 | *The Properties of Unnatural Darkness* | Investigation deepens. Mirrors crack. Animals flee. Kalmaris sees something in a reflection. Lufnik notices water behaving oddly. | Foreshadowing planted: mirror, water, shadows. |
| 5 | *On the Subject of Teamwork (A Preliminary Study)* | Party must work together for the first time. Personalities clash. Serox and Longjohn argue. Lamil mediates with three words. | Character dynamics comedy. Serox's "obviously" tic. |
| 6 | *The Thing Beneath the Island* | Shadow manifestations attack. Real danger. First combat -- emotional, not tactical. The shadow dragon is HINTED. | Shift from comedy to genuine threat. Earns the tonal range. |

### Act 2: The Mystery of Moreva (Chapters 7-13, ~17,500 words)
*Rising Action: 25%*

**Goal**: Deepen the mystery, develop relationships, escalate danger.

| Ch | Title (Working) | Content | Key Beats |
|----|----------------|---------|-----------|
| 7 | *Hypothesis and Hazard* | Milo forms a theory. Party splits to investigate. Each pair reveals character through investigation style. | Kalmaris/Milo pair: comedy. Longjohn/Serox pair: friction. Lamil/Lufnik: unexpected connection. |
| 8 | *What the Shadows Remember* | Discovery: the madness is feeding something underground. Locals' memories are being eaten. | Horror element. Lamil feels the wrongness in nature. |
| 9 | *The Bard's Reflection* | Kalmaris's reflection lingers in a pool. First real Gargauth seed -- subtle, dismissible. Meanwhile, Milo's cooking poisons a vine creature (accidentally useful). | Kalmaris foreshadowing. Cooking-as-weapon running gag seed. |
| 10 | *A Brief Treatise on Trust* | Lufnik almost reveals his powers in an emergency. Covers it up. Milo notices a "localized atmospheric anomaly." | Lufnik's secret. Milo's oblivious observation. |
| 11 | *The Cave Network* | Exploration of Moreva's underground. Maps, traps, ancient murals. The dragon's lair is found but not breached. | Dungeon exploration played for discovery, not combat. |
| 12 | *Serox's Assessment* | Serox evaluates the group. Blunt, accurate, secretly affectionate assessments of each person. His armor philosophy introduced. | Character study chapter. Serox reveals depth under bluntness. |
| 13 | *The Dragon's Shadow* | Confrontation with the shadow dragon's presence. Not the dragon itself -- its influence. A party member is briefly controlled. | Stakes raised. Personal vulnerability. |

### Act 3: Midpoint Reversal (Chapters 14-16, ~7,500 words)
*Midpoint: 10%*

| Ch | Title (Working) | Content | Key Beats |
|----|----------------|---------|-----------|
| 14 | *The Scientific Method (Applied to Dragon-Slaying)* | Milo plans the fight using alchemical logic. Each person has a role based on their strengths. | Sanderson-style: magic system used cleverly. |
| 15 | *Fire and Shadow* | The battle with the shadow dragon. Emotional stakes: protecting the villagers, protecting each other. Lamil's wildfire vs. shadow. | Climactic but not the REAL climax. Victory with cost. |
| 16 | *The Morning After* | Celebration. Bonding. The RR-Guild's letter arrives for Milo. He's intrigued but stays. Seeds of Volume 2. | Warm chapter. Earned rest. Plant future conflict. |

### Act 4: Ice and Stone (Chapters 17-24, ~20,000 words)
*Escalation: 30%*

| Ch | Title (Working) | Content | Key Beats |
|----|----------------|---------|-----------|
| 17 | *Regarding the Thermal Properties of Regret* | Mountain crossing begins. Cold. Griffins. Narrator's prose contracts with the cold. | Environmental storytelling. Prose mirrors physical state. |
| 18 | *The White Dragon's Domain* | Avoiding a white dragon. Stealth sequence. Longjohn is suspiciously quiet about Blacky's nervousness. | Tension. Blacky as early warning system. |
| 19 | *Hospitality (Dwarven Definition)* | Arriving in Zornstein. Grudging welcome. Beer. Serox is HOME. | Worldbuilding through character joy. Serox lights up. |
| 20 | *The Iron Aesthete Returns* | Serox at the forge. His philosophy of perfection. First armor piece crafted. Meanwhile, the city is getting hotter. | Serox's depth. B-plot: heating crisis. |
| 21 | *On the Subject of Dwarven Denial* | The heating crisis is real and getting worse. The dwarves insist it's fine. Serox insists it's fine. Milo measures the temperature. | Comedy: institutional denial. Serox embodies dwarven culture. |
| 22 | *Mine Cart Metaphysics* | Descent into the mines. Discovery of the fire god's temple. Lava lake. Ancient rituals. | Adventure. Worldbuilding. The mine cart ride is genuinely fun. |
| 23 | *The Trapped God Speaks* | Meeting the imprisoned fire deity. Learning about the magma elemental. The stakes: if it completes its power theft, Zornstein is destroyed. | Exposition through dialogue. The fire god is dignified and sad. |
| 24 | *Lufnik Falls* | Lufnik attempts an acrobatic crossing and falls toward lava. Party rescues him. His water powers activate involuntarily. He's saved but traumatized. | Lufnik's fire phobia origin. Water powers visible but unexplained. |

### Act 5: The Cost of Victory (Chapters 25-30, ~12,500 words)
*Climax: 15%*

| Ch | Title (Working) | Content | Key Beats |
|----|----------------|---------|-----------|
| 25 | *The Ritual Interrupted* | Party disrupts the elemental's power-transfer ritual. Magma elemental summoned. | Point of no return. |
| 26 | *The Island in the Fire* | Battle on a rock platform in the lava lake. Desperate survival. The fire god helps but is weakened. | Emotional combat. |
| 27 | *Blacky* | **The chapter.** Blacky charges the elemental to protect Longjohn. Blacky dies. Longjohn's grief. The elemental is defeated at this cost. | Emotional climax. No jokes. No deflection. Let it hurt. |
| 28 | *What Remains* | Aftermath. Longjohn's silence. The party doesn't know what to do. Milo tries to help with science and fails -- because grief isn't a problem to solve. | Milo's arc: confronting the limits of analysis. |
| 29 | *The Forge and the Letter* | Serox completes his first armor piece. Blobby arrives with Milo's letter warning of trouble. The party decides to go to Baldur's Gate. | Recovery. New direction. Purpose from loss. |
| 30 | *The Gnome Who Knew Too Little* | Final chapter. Milo reflects. The party sets out. **CLIFFHANGER**: RR-Guild agents ambush. Milo is taken. Blobby barely escapes with a message. | Cliffhanger. Title explained: Milo didn't know enough to see the betrayal coming. |
