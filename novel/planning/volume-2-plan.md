# Volume 2: "The City of Reasonable Knives"

**Target**: ~87,500 words | 35 chapters | ~2,500 words/chapter

---

## Premise

Milo's companions race to Baldur's Gate to rescue him from the RR-Guild. What follows is a tangled web of city politics, faction intrigue, underground arena fights, a heist-like auction infiltration, and the discovery that Milo's kidnapping was just one thread in a much larger conspiracy. Meanwhile, Kalmaris gets cursed, loses his identity, and starts hearing whispers in mirrors. The rescue succeeds but the cost is high -- and on the road out of the city, Elturel vanishes into Hell.

## Key Adaptation Changes

- **The auction sequence is expanded** into a multi-chapter heist/infiltration arc (Chapters 18-24)
- **Blobby's behavior changes subtly** starting around Chapter 21, foreshadowing the doppelganger
- **The doppelganger is established earlier** with hints, not just revealed in the boss fight
- **Milo captivity interludes** alternate with the main party's chapters, using the omniscient narrator to follow both threads

---

## Narrative POV Structure

The omniscient narrator follows both storylines: the party's rescue mission and Milo's captivity. Milo's chapters are shorter (1,500-2,000 words) and more claustrophobic -- the narrator's usually expansive voice contracts in the cell, mirroring Milo's confinement. Milo's journal entries (blue boxes) become his primary emotional outlet during these chapters.

Pattern: 3 party chapters, 1 Milo chapter, repeat.

### Act 3: The Auction (Chapters 18-24, ~17,500 words)

The auction is a multi-chapter heist sequence. Each character has a role.

| Ch | Title (Working) | Content | Key Beats |
|----|----------------|---------|-----------|
| 18 | *Preparations (A Heist in Several Parts)* | Planning the auction infiltration. Roles assigned. Disguises. Intel gathering. The clock is ticking for Kalmaris. | Heist-movie energy. Each character plays to their strength. |
| 19 | *The Bronze Tier* | Kalmaris gets invited to the Bronze tier after buying Liquid Sunlight. Discovers connections to Strahd and the forest vampires. | Plot convergence. Old threads reconnect. |
| 20 | *The Floor* | The auction itself begins. Exotic items. Factional bidding wars. Political maneuvering as entertainment. | Worldbuilding. Comedy: Serox evaluating every item's craftsmanship. |
| 21 | *Interlude: Blobby's Last Day* | The narrator shows Milo's last conscious moments before the doppelganger takes over. He sees Blobby change. The prose is the most clinical and the most terrified it's ever been near Milo. | The shift. After this, the "Milo" the party will find is fake. |
| 22 | *The Theft* | Auction attack. An organization tries to steal the final item. Combat and chaos. Deaths of Kaelin and Garrick. | Action set piece. Consequences: named characters die. |
| 23 | *Aftermath and Information* | RR-Guild thanks the party. Reveals Milo's location. But the party notices Blobby acting... different. Subtle. | Doppelganger seed planted. The reader should feel uneasy. |
| 24 | *The Plan* | Rescue planning. Blobby's behavior is slightly off -- different reactions, different sounds. Milo would notice. The party doesn't. | Dramatic irony. The reader knows more than the characters. |
