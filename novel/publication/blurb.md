# Blurb & Synopsis

## Royal Road Blurb

Milo Quickspark is a gnome alchemist with two great loves: the pursuit of knowledge and cooking that could, in a pinch, be classified as a war crime.

When a shipwreck strands him on an island where the locals are losing their minds -- and not because of his cooking, for once -- he finds himself stuck with five companions who have no business forming a functional group: a bird-man bard having an identity crisis, a druid who speaks more to trees than people, a soldier hiding powers he doesn't understand, a grieving pirate who drinks like it's a competitive sport, and a dwarf who would rather eat his own hammer than admit he was wrong.

Together, they'll fight shadow dragons, navigate city politics, infiltrate a secret auction, and eventually descend into literal Hell. Along the way, Milo will take meticulous notes, brew questionable potions, and discover that the most volatile element in any experiment is friendship.

(The second most volatile element is still fluorine. Some things are universal.)

## Extended Synopsis (for author use, not publication)

**Volume 1 -- The Gnome Who Knew Too Little**: Six strangers shipwrecked on a mysterious island band together to solve a shadow dragon mystery, cross frozen mountains, and survive a dwarven city threatened by a magma elemental. One of them loses his beloved companion. Another is kidnapped.

**Volume 2 -- The City of Reasonable Knives**: The party races to Baldur's Gate to rescue their alchemist. City politics, underground arena fights, a multi-chapter auction heist, and the discovery that one of their members is being possessed by an ancient devil. They rescue Milo just in time to watch Elturel vanish into Hell.

**Volume 3 -- A Somewhat Unreliable Guide to Hell**: The party descends into Avernus to rescue a city. What they find is a burning wasteland of eternal war, screaming currency, and corrupted magic. Each character faces a personal reckoning with a terrible price. Somewhere in the sulfurous dark, a five-headed shadow waits.

## Cover Description

**Concept**: A gnome in alchemist's goggles sitting on a rock, writing in a notebook, with an amorphous blob creature (Blobby) on his shoulder. Behind him, slightly out of focus, the other five party members are engaged in various stages of chaos: Kalmaris gesturing dramatically, Serox examining something with disapproval, Longjohn drinking, Lamil standing perfectly still near a single surviving plant, and Lufnik standing at attention while a mysterious puddle forms at his feet.

**Color palette**: Warm golds and browns (parchment tones) for the foreground, transitioning to darker reds and oranges (hellfire) in the background -- hinting at where the story is going.

**Typography**: Title in a serif font with alchemical flourishes. Author name below in clean sans-serif.
