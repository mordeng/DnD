# Royal Road Publication Strategy

## Pre-Launch Phase

### Buffer
Build a **10-chapter buffer** before publishing the first chapter. This provides:
- Consistency in early posting schedule (crucial for Royal Road algorithm)
- Room to revise early chapters based on later developments
- Protection against writer's block or life interruptions during launch

### Pre-Launch Tasks
- [ ] Write chapters 1-10 of Volume 1
- [ ] Revise chapters 1-5 (the first impression chapters)
- [ ] Write blurb (see `blurb.md`)
- [ ] Prepare cover description / commission cover art
- [ ] Set up Royal Road profile and author bio
- [ ] Select tags and content warnings
- [ ] Write author's notes for chapters 1-10
- [ ] Prepare 3-4 "blue box specials" for interstitial content

---

## Launch Plan

### Day 1: Launch with 5 Chapters
Post chapters 1-5 simultaneously. This gives new readers enough to hook them and decide to follow.

**Why 5**: Royal Road's Rising Stars list favors stories with early page views and follows. Multiple chapters on Day 1 maximizes first-impression engagement.

### Weeks 1-4: 3x/Week
Post Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Consistent schedule builds reader habit.

### Week 5+: 2x/Week
Drop to Tuesday, Friday. Sustainable long-term pace. Maintain buffer.

## Author's Notes

Every chapter gets an author's note. These build community and reader loyalty.

**Length**: 100-200 words
**Tone**: Warm, conversational, grateful

**Content rotation**:
- Behind-the-scenes (what inspired the chapter, a D&D campaign tidbit)
- Reader question (ask about predictions, favorite characters, etc.)
- Tease for next chapter (without spoilers)
- Craft observation (briefly discuss a writing decision)
- Gratitude (thank readers, acknowledge milestones)

**Things to avoid**:
- Apologizing for the chapter
- Promising things you can't deliver
- Spoiling future content
- Complaining about writing difficulty
- Self-deprecation beyond light humor

## Interstitial Content

Between major arcs (roughly every 10-15 chapters), publish a "blue box special" -- standalone in-world content that isn't a chapter but enriches the world:

### Types
1. **Extended Guide Entries**: Longer "Somewhat Unreliable Guide" essays (500-1,000 words)
2. **Alchemist's Research Notes**: Milo's extended observations on a topic
3. **Letters**: Correspondence between characters or to/from NPCs
4. **Song Lyrics**: Kalmaris's songs (if well-written; if not, skip)
5. **Serox's Equipment Reviews**: Blunt evaluations of items encountered (comedy)

These are marked as "Interlude" or "Bonus" chapters on Royal Road.

## Engagement Tactics

### Reader Interaction
- Respond to comments on every chapter (at least the first 20-30)
- Ask specific questions in author's notes to prompt discussion
- Create polls for minor decisions (not plot-affecting)
- Acknowledge reader theories (without confirming or denying)

### Rising Stars Optimization
- Consistent posting schedule is the single most important factor
- Launch with multiple chapters
- Engage with other authors (read, rate, review)
- Clean tags and accurate blurb attract the right audience
- First chapter must hook within 500 words

### Retention
- Every chapter ends with a hook
- Every arc resolves enough to satisfy while opening new questions
- Balance episodic satisfaction (something happens each chapter) with serial tension (the bigger story progresses)

## Tags & Categories

**Primary Tags**: Fantasy, Comedy, Adventure, Multiple Lead Characters

**Secondary Tags**: Magic, Found Family, Male Lead, Low Fantasy (mortal-scale problems even in cosmic settings)

**Content Warnings**: Mild violence, character death (non-graphic), mild horror elements (Blobulus, soul coins)

## Long-Term Considerations

- **Patreon/Ko-fi**: Consider after 200+ followers. Offer 5-chapter advance access.
- **Cover art**: Commission professional cover after proving the concept (50+ followers)
- **Editing**: First pass self-edit. Consider beta readers after Volume 1 is drafted.
- **Print/ebook**: Not relevant until all 3 volumes are complete and edited.
