# Master Story Bible

## Logline

A gnome alchemist whose cooking qualifies as a war crime stumbles through shipwrecks, city politics, and literal Hell alongside five companions who have no business being friends -- discovering along the way that the most volatile element in any experiment is friendship. (The second most volatile element is still fluorine. Some things are universal.)

## Tone Statement

- **40% Pratchett**: Warm satire, footnotes that reveal character, institutional absurdity with a human heart. The joke is never cruel.
- **35% Adams**: Cosmic absurdism, deadpan catastrophe, bureaucratic horror played straight. The universe is vast and indifferent, which is somehow hilarious.
- **25% Sanderson**: Hard magic systems with clear costs, planted details that pay off satisfyingly, escalation that earns its climax. The rules matter because breaking them means something.

## Core Promise

"You will laugh, you will care about these idiots, and the magic will make sense."

## Thematic Throughlines

### Primary Theme: Identity
- Who are you when everything that defined you is stripped away?
- Kalmaris loses his pirate identity. Lufnik questions his resurrection. Milo was replaced by a doppelganger. Longjohn lost his companion. Even Serox's definition of "perfection" evolves.

### Secondary Theme: Found Family
- Six people who have no business being friends become inseparable
- The cost of caring about people in a world that punishes attachment
- "The most volatile element in any experiment is friendship"

### Tertiary Theme: The Cost of Power
- Every magic system has a price. Every shortcut has a consequence.
- Soul coins literalize the economy of suffering
- Gargauth offers power that feeds on the user
- The "perfect armor" requires imperfect choices

## Point of View

**Third Person Omniscient** -- a Pratchett-style narrator who knows everything, sees everything, and is deeply fond of these idiots. The narrator is a distinct voice: warm, witty, slightly exasperated. Can zoom from cosmic to intimate. Adjusts prose style subtly when close to different characters (scientific near Milo, sparse near Lamil, etc.). Milo's first-person analytical voice is preserved through journal excerpts embedded as blue boxes.

## Narrative Structure

Each volume follows a 5-act structure:
1. **Setup** (20%): Establish world, characters, stakes
2. **Rising Action** (25%): Complicate, deepen, reveal
3. **Midpoint Reversal** (10%): Everything changes
4. **Escalation** (30%): Raise stakes, converge threads
5. **Climax & Resolution** (15%): Pay off everything planted
