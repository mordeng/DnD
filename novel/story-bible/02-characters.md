# Character Bible

## Milo Quickspark -- The Volatile Element

**Race**: Gnome | **Role**: Alchemist & Researcher | **Age**: ~80 (young adult for a gnome)

### Personality & Backstory
Born in Eldertree Glade, Milo showed an early talent for tinkering and an unshakeable love for cooking (which unfortunately did not include any talent for it). Apprenticed under Eldra Brightflask, a master alchemist who taught him discipline he has yet to fully internalize. Moved to Baldur's Gate, joined the Alchemist's Consortium (later: Mechanists' Collective), and was kidnapped by the RR-Guild for mysterious experiments. Rescued by the party. His familiar Blobby is a small, amorphous creature of uncertain taxonomy who translates the world through wobbles, color changes, and enthusiastic bouncing.

### Novel Voice (Dialogue & Journal Entries)
- **Sentence patterns**: Long compound sentences when comfortable (intellectual pleasure). Short staccato when afraid. Scientific precision always, escalating with emotional intensity.
- **Vocabulary domain**: Chemistry, biology, physics, culinary arts. "Exothermic" instead of "hot." "Structural integrity" instead of "strength."
- **Verbal tics**: "From a purely [X] standpoint..." / "Which, in retrospect..."
- **Journal excerpts**: Milo keeps a journal throughout. Excerpts appear as blue boxes in his first-person scientific voice -- the primary window into his inner monologue. These are alchemical observations, notes-to-self, and increasingly desperate personal reflections.
- **Narrator proximity**: When the omniscient narrator is close to Milo, the prose itself becomes more scientific and precise, colored by his worldview.

### Emotional Tell
When Milo is emotional, he becomes MORE scientific, not less -- visible in his dialogue and journal entries. Peak terror sounds like a chemistry lecture. Grief is described through the molecular composition of tears. Love is expressed as concern about someone's vitamin intake. The omniscient narrator can also observe this pattern and comment on it.

### Character Arc
**Start**: Chaotic researcher who treats the world as a fascinating experiment and people as variables
**End**: Still a chaotic researcher, but one who understands that some variables are worth protecting with everything you have
**Cost**: Must accept that his need to understand everything is sometimes a wall he hides behind

### Sample Dialogue
> "I want to be absolutely clear that the fire was a controlled experiment. The fact that it became uncontrolled does not change the initial intent, which was entirely scientific. Serox, please stop growling. It's counterproductive and, from a purely cardiovascular standpoint, bad for your blood pressure."

### Running Gags
1. **Terrible cooking**: His cooking is catastrophically bad. He is genuinely, blissfully unaware of this. The party has developed elaborate systems to avoid his meals without hurting his feelings. Blobby is the only creature that eats his food willingly, which says more about Blobby's biology than Milo's cooking.
2. **Blobby translations**: Milo interprets Blobby's wobbles, color shifts, and bouncing patterns with absolute confidence. Whether these translations are accurate is entirely unclear. Milo has never questioned the methodology.

## Kalmaris -- The Unmoored Bard

**Race**: Aarakocra (bird-person) | **Role**: Bard & Former Pirate | **Age**: ~12 (adult for Aarakocra)

### Personality & Backstory
Born on sea cliffs, drawn to adventure. Swept overboard as a youth and taken in by the pirate crew of the Emerald Crow. Became their bard, singing songs of freedom and plunder. In Baldur's Gate, cursed by a Fate Card and possessed by a mirror demon -- later revealed to be an echo of Gargauth, the Lord of Betrayers. A doctor sealed the demon but stripped Kalmaris of his pirate identity in the process. He now struggles with who he is without the only life he's known.

### Novel Voice
- **Sentence patterns**: Theatrical, dramatic, performer's cadence with deliberate pauses. When vulnerable, drops to raw, simple sentences -- almost childlike.
- **Vocabulary domain**: Music, theater, the sea. Describes emotions as songs ("This feels like a minor key"). Uses dramatic metaphors that are slightly too elaborate.
- **Verbal tics**: Dramatic pauses. Refers to situations as "acts" or "scenes." Occasional song lyrics mid-conversation.

### Emotional Tell
When truly vulnerable, his theatrical vocabulary vanishes entirely. "I don't know who I am" hits harder because every other sentence is ornate.

### Character Arc
**Start**: A performer without a performance -- lost identity, covering pain with showmanship
**End**: Discovers that identity isn't what you were, but what you choose. Must face Gargauth's corruption and decide who he becomes
**Cost**: Every use of Gargauth's gifted powers feeds the demon; choosing himself may mean choosing weakness

### Sample Dialogue
> "My dear, small, combustible friend -- and I mean that with the utmost affection -- perhaps the next time you decide to 'improve' breakfast, you might consider that some of us have taste buds that have not yet been cauterized by years of alchemical exposure."

> *(vulnerable)*: "They took it. My name, my crew, my songs. What's left?"

### Running Gag
Becomes violently angry when anyone mispronounces his name as "Calamari." The frequency of this mispronunciation is statistically improbable, which Milo has noted in his journal.

## Lamil Morgenhauch -- The Quiet Flame

**Race**: Elf | **Role**: Druid (Circle of Wildfire) | **Age**: ~200

### Personality & Backstory
Born in the Eldariel Forest. A member and leader of the Ember Brotherhood (Glutbruderschaft), guardians of the Wildfire Flame. His former friend Morgath Dunkelschatten was corrupted by dark magic and perverted the Wildfire arts. Lamil is deeply connected to nature and profoundly uncomfortable around civilization. He communicates with nature spirits and reads the health of the world through the behavior of wind and water.

### Novel Voice
- **Sentence patterns**: Sparse. Few words. Nature metaphors for everything. When he trusts someone, his sentences get longer -- this is the single clearest indicator of his emotional state.
- **Vocabulary domain**: Flora, fauna, weather, seasons. Refers to people as plant or animal types ("You are an oak" = respect. "You are bindweed" = contempt.)
- **Verbal tics**: Long silences that are not awkward for him but are for everyone else. Occasionally talks to plants mid-conversation.

### Emotional Tell
Sentence length. Three-word sentences from Lamil are normal. A full paragraph means something profound is happening.

### Character Arc
**Start**: Withdrawn protector who trusts trees more than people
**End**: Still trusts trees, but has learned that some people are worth the same faith
**Cost**: In Avernus, his wildfire is corrupted by infernal influence -- he must choose between his principles and his power

### Sample Dialogue
> "Fire cleanses. This fire does not. It consumes."

> *(trusting)*: "When I was young, there was a tree in the Eldariel that had been struck by lightning seven times. Each time, it grew back differently. Not weaker. Not stronger. Just... different. I think of that tree sometimes, when I look at you."

### Running Gag
Deeply uncomfortable in cities. Will find the single patch of grass in any urban environment and gravitate toward it. Has been known to apologize to potted plants for their captivity.

## Lufnik Springveil -- The Still Water

**Race**: Human | **Role**: Soldier / Secret Water Mage | **Age**: ~28

### Personality & Backstory
A soldier from a lakeside village, Lufnik died in battle alongside his best friend Arian Brightwater. He prayed to the moon and was resurrected with the ability to control water -- a power he keeps secret out of fear. His mentor Aldric and friend Arian are both missing since the war. He has a military bearing, formal speech, and the disciplined habit of showing no emotion -- except that water near him betrays his feelings.

### Novel Voice
- **Sentence patterns**: Military precision. Subject-verb-object. Clipped. Formal even in casual settings.
- **Vocabulary domain**: Military terminology applied to everything. "Tactical assessment" for looking around. "Reconnaissance" for asking questions.
- **Verbal tics**: "Acknowledged." "Understood." "Noted." Reports situations rather than discussing them.

### Emotional Tell
Water near him responds to his emotions. Puddles ripple. Cups overflow. Rain bends toward him. He doesn't realize this is visible to others -- it's his most closely guarded secret leaking out anyway. Milo notices but initially attributes it to "localized atmospheric anomalies."

### Character Arc
**Start**: A disciplined soldier hiding a power he doesn't understand, terrified of being seen as something other than normal
**End**: Accepts his power and the moon's gift, even when a dark patron offers a corrupted alternative
**Cost**: In Avernus, with no moon, his power source is cut off -- the temptation to accept a dark patron's offer becomes real

### Sample Dialogue
> "Current threat assessment: one fire elemental, estimated height twelve meters, composed primarily of magma with a core temperature exceeding-- Milo, stop taking notes and run."

### Running Gag
Inexplicable puddles appear around him at emotional moments. No one can figure out where the water comes from. In Avernus (where water shouldn't exist), this becomes increasingly conspicuous and increasingly difficult to explain.

## Longjohn Silver -- The Grieving Storm

**Race**: Human (Air Genasi heritage) | **Role**: Beast Master & Former Pirate | **Age**: ~35

### Personality & Backstory
Born in the floating citadel of Airspire, joined the pirate vessel Galeheart. A scarred captain with a distinctive mark over his left eye massacred his crew and left him for dead. Marooned on an island, he killed a mother boar for food and adopted her orphaned piglet, Blacky, out of guilt and loneliness. Blacky became his inseparable companion, best friend, and reason to keep going. Blacky died fighting the magma elemental in the dwarf city. Longjohn has not recovered. He drinks to forget and hunts the scarred captain for revenge.

### Novel Voice
- **Sentence patterns**: Nautical idioms applied to every situation ("the wind's changed," "we're sailing into rough waters," "that's a lee shore"). Rough, direct, poetic in an untutored way.
- **Vocabulary domain**: The sea, weather, ships, knots, tides. Everything is a sailing metaphor.
- **Verbal tics**: Goes completely, dangerously silent when Blacky is mentioned. The absence of his usual bluster is louder than any scream.

### Emotional Tell
Silence. Longjohn talks constantly -- blustering, boasting, complaining. When he stops, something is deeply wrong. His silence about Blacky is the loudest thing in any room.

### Character Arc
**Start**: A man running from grief into revenge, using anger as a life raft
**End**: Learns that grief doesn't end, but it can become something other than rage -- possibly by rescuing Blacky's soul in Hell, possibly by learning to let go
**Cost**: The scarred captain may offer him revenge at a terrible price, and Blacky's rescue may require a sacrifice

### Sample Dialogue
> "The way I see it, there's two kinds of storms: the ones that break you and the ones that carry you somewhere new. This one--" *gestures at the chaos* "--I haven't decided yet."

> *(about Blacky)*: "..."

### Running Gag
Drinks with the commitment of someone training for a competition. Has opinions about every alcoholic beverage. Can identify a drink's origin, vintage, and moral character by smell alone.

## Serox Kieselstein -- The Immovable Object

**Race**: Dwarf | **Role**: Armorer / Craftsman | **Age**: ~120

### Personality & Backstory
Born in Zornstein, the mountain kingdom where smithing is sacred. Obsessed with crafting the perfect armor -- not just protective but beautiful. Left home to seek rare materials and techniques across the world. Known as "the Iron Aesthete." Has never admitted to being wrong about anything, ever, under any circumstances. Will construct elaborate logical frameworks to justify positions that are clearly, obviously, spectacularly incorrect.

### Novel Voice
- **Sentence patterns**: Blunt declarations. No hedging, no qualifiers, no "maybe." Speaks in absolutes.
- **Vocabulary domain**: Metallurgy, engineering, structural analysis. Evaluates everything by its material properties. People are "well-tempered" or "brittle." Situations are "load-bearing" or "structurally unsound."
- **Verbal tics**: "Obviously." "As I said." "I have already explained this." (He has not.)

### Emotional Tell
When wrong -- which he never admits -- he becomes more eloquent, not less. The quality of his justification is directly proportional to how wrong he is. A brief "obviously" means he's probably right. A three-paragraph treatise on metallurgical theory means he just broke something.

### Character Arc
**Start**: A perfectionist who defines himself by his work and refuses to acknowledge flaws in himself or his craft
**End**: Discovers that the perfect armor protects the people you love, not the person wearing it -- and that admitting imperfection is not weakness
**Cost**: In Avernus, soul coins offer the ultimate crafting material at an unconscionable moral price

### Sample Dialogue
> "The wall collapsed because of pre-existing structural deficiencies in the masonry, not because I hit it with my hammer. The fact that I hit it with my hammer is, at best, a coincidence. At worst, it revealed a flaw that would have manifested eventually regardless."

### Running Gag
Would rather eat his own hammer than admit he was wrong. Has constructed increasingly baroque justifications for obvious errors. The party has started keeping a tally. Current score: Serox Wrong But Admitting It: 0. Serox Wrong But Explaining Why He's Actually Right: 147.
