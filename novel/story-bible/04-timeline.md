# Novel Timeline

This timeline diverges from the D&D campaign. See `../planning/adaptation-notes.md` for specific changes and reasons.

## Volume 1: "The Gnome Who Knew Too Little"
*Approximately Days 1-67 of the journey*

### Act 1: Shipwreck & Shadow (Chapters 1-6)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 1 | Shipwreck on Moreva | Party meets on the beach. Milo is WITH the group (unlike campaign). |
| 1-3 | Jungle survival | Establish character dynamics, Milo's cooking gag, Blobby introduction |
| 4-7 | Village discovery | Locals acting strangely -- madness spreading. Milo begins investigating. |
| 8-10 | Shadow clues planted | Subtle wrongness: shadows move independently, mirrors crack, animals flee |

### Act 2: The Mystery Deepens (Chapters 7-13)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 11-15 | Investigation arc | Milo leads scientific inquiry into the madness. Party splits to gather clues. |
| 16-18 | First confrontation | Shadow manifestations attack. The dragon is hinted but not revealed. |
| 19-21 | Kalmaris's mirror | First subtle sign of the mirror curse -- a reflection that lingers too long |

### Act 3: Midpoint Reversal (Chapters 14-16)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 22-23 | Shadow dragon revealed | The mystery resolves into a boss: the shadow dragon beneath Moreva |
| 23 | Battle of Moreva | Party defeats the shadow dragon. Victory celebration. |
| 23 | RR-Guild invitation | Milo receives the research offer. Plants the seed but does NOT leave yet. |

### Act 4: Into the Mountains (Chapters 17-24)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 24-30 | Mountain crossing begins | Griffin encounter. White dragon avoidance. Cold as a character. |
| 31-35 | Frost wolves & dwarven patrol | Party discovered by Zornstein scouts |
| 36-45 | Dwarf city arc | Serox's first armor piece. Crafting. The heating crisis. |
| 46-50 | Mine cart descent | Discovery of the fire god's temple and the magma elemental |

### Act 5: Fire & Loss (Chapters 24-30)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 51-52 | Lufnik's fall | Lufnik falls into lava (Ch 24, Act 4 finale). Survives but develops fire phobia. Water powers first hint. |
| 56-60 | Ritual interruption | Party disrupts the power-transfer ritual. Magma elemental summoned. |
| 61-65 | The Battle of the Lava Lake | **Blacky dies** protecting Longjohn. Emotional climax of Volume 1. |
| 66-67 | Aftermath | Serox forges his first piece. Milo's letter arrives via Blobby. |
| 67 | **CLIFFHANGER**: Milo is taken | Milo is kidnapped by RR-Guild agents. Blobby barely escapes to warn the party. |

## Volume 3: "A Somewhat Unreliable Guide to Hell"
*Day 135 onward*

### Act 1: Descent (Chapters 1-8)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 135-140 | Journey to the crater | Undead attack. Zodge's briefing. Decision to descend. |
| 141-145 | Entering Avernus | First impressions. Environmental shock. Milo begins writing "The Guide." |
| 146-150 | Hellturel | The chained city. Survivors. The corrupted Companion. High Hall exploration. |

### Act 2: Survival (Chapters 9-20)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 151-160 | Fort Knucklebone | Mad Maggie. War Machine acquisition. Soul coin introduction. |
| 161-170 | Hex crawl sequence | Travel mechanics. Encounters. Brimstone Bill. Wandering Emporium. |
| 171-180 | Personal quest seeds | Each character's Avernus quest begins to activate |
| 181-190 | Lufnik's crisis | No moon. Powers failing. Dark patron makes contact. |

### Act 3: Midpoint -- The Blood War (Chapters 21-24)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 191-200 | Caught in a Blood War skirmish | The party witnesses the war's true scale. Stakes become cosmic. |
| 200 | Tarrasque sighting | Brief, terrifying, humbling. A reminder of scale. |

### Act 4: The Reckoning (Chapters 25-35)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 201-210 | Longjohn finds Blacky's soul coin | Blacky's soul found. Devils offer to free Blacky at a terrible price. (Ch 25-26) |
| 211-215 | Serox at the forge | Soul coin armor vs. pure craftsmanship. Makes his choice. (Ch 27) |
| 216-220 | Lamil's flame | Confrontation with corrupted fire. Chooses his magic's nature. (Ch 28) |
| 221-225 | Lufnik's moon | Accepts or rejects the dark patron. Reveals his powers to the party. (Ch 29) |
| 226-230 | Kalmaris's mirror | Makes his choice about Gargauth. Three options, none free. (Ch 30) |
| 231-240 | Tiamat convergence | Five colors, five shadows converge. Milo sees the pattern. (Ch 32-33) |
| 241-250 | Climax | The party faces Tiamat's influence together. All magic systems pay off. (Ch 34-35) |

### Act 5: Going Home (Chapters 36-40)
| Day | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 251-255 | Leaving Avernus | Each character reflects on what they gained and lost. (Ch 36) |
| 256-257 | Milo's cooking | Celebration dinner. The party eats every bite without complaint. (Ch 37) |
| 258-259 | Letters sent | Milo's Hell-letters finally delivered. Guild responds. (Ch 38) |
| 260 | Final Guide entry | "Why Go to Hell?" -- thematic statement as comedy. (Ch 39) |
| 260+ | The Alchemist's Logbook | Milo closes his journal. Series end. Open threads for future stories. (Ch 40) |
