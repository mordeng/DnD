# Factions & Organizations

## Mortal World Factions

### The Flaming Fists
**Type**: Mercenary company / City guard | **Base**: Baldur's Gate | **Alignment**: Pragmatic neutral

The largest military force on the Sword Coast, serving as both army and police for Baldur's Gate. Led by the Grand Duke, with Captain Zodge as the party's primary contact. Hierarchical, well-equipped, and morally flexible in the way that institutions become when they have too much power and too little oversight.

**Novel role**: Represent institutional authority -- not evil, but not good either. They enforce order, which sometimes means enforcing injustice. Captain Zodge is pragmatic and mission-focused; he uses the party because they're useful, not because he cares about them.

**Key NPCs**: Captain Zodge (pragmatic commander, assigns Avernus mission)

---

### The Azure-Enklave
**Type**: Mage guild | **Base**: Baldur's Gate | **Alignment**: Neutral (knowledge-focused)

A secretive organization of mages and scholars. Highly selective membership. Extensive library, powerful artifacts, and an institutional belief that knowledge is power and sharing knowledge is weakness. Their true goals are unclear even to most members.

**Novel role**: Information gatekeepers. The party accesses them through Lamil's nature-magic credentials (druids and mages share academic respect). They provide exposition and resources but always at a price -- usually information in return.

---

### The RR-Guild (Rebellische Racher)
**Type**: Resistance movement | **Base**: Baldur's Gate (hidden) | **Alignment**: Ostensibly good

A secret organization fighting corruption and protecting the common people. Operates in cells for security. Noble mission, questionable methods. They kidnapped Milo for mysterious experiments -- their "good" cause doesn't excuse their means.

**Novel role**: Antagonists in Volume 2, but sympathetic ones. They believe they're saving the world. They might even be right. They're still the people who held Milo prisoner and created the Blobulus. The moral complexity matters.

**Key NPCs**: Cell leaders (unnamed, shadowy), lab researchers

---

### The Mechanists' Collective (Alchemist's Consortium)
**Type**: Artisan guild | **Base**: Baldur's Gate | **Alignment**: Chaotic good

Milo's guild. Alchemists, tinkerers, inventors, and enthusiasts united by a love of blowing things up in the pursuit of knowledge. Disorganized, enthusiastic, and perpetually covered in soot.

**Novel role**: Milo's emotional anchor to the mortal world. They represent what he could be if he stayed safe -- comfortable, productive, and not in Hell. They also represent the community he's fighting to return to.

---

### The Ember Brotherhood (Glutbruderschaft)
**Type**: Druid circle | **Base**: Eldariel Forest | **Alignment**: Neutral good

Lamil's organization. Druids of the Circle of Wildfire who use fire as a force of renewal, not destruction. Ancient traditions, deep connection to nature spirits, and a complicated relationship with civilization.

**Novel role**: Lamil's backstory anchor. Their philosophy (fire cleanses, fire renews) becomes thematically critical in Avernus, where fire is corrupted. Letters from the Brotherhood may reach Lamil with guidance -- or warnings.

---

### The Liberation Brigade (Befreiungsbrigade)
**Type**: Rebel army | **Base**: Various | **Alignment**: Chaotic good

Lufnik's former comrades. Rebels fighting occupying forces. Most of them think Lufnik is dead. His return would be complicated -- a resurrected soldier with secret powers is either a miracle or a threat, depending on your perspective.

**Novel role**: Background for Lufnik. May appear in Volume 3 if former comrades ended up in Hell.

---

## Infernal Factions

### Zariel's Army
**Type**: Devil military | **Base**: Bronze Citadel, Avernus | **Alignment**: Lawful evil

The standing army of Avernus, commanded by Zariel herself -- a fallen angel who became what she swore to destroy. Highly organized, mercilessly efficient, and locked in eternal war with the demonic hordes of the Abyss.

**Novel role**: The dominant power in Avernus. Zariel is a tragic figure -- once a champion of good, now a tyrant of Hell. She represents the corruption theme at its most extreme: what happens when you fight evil for so long that you become it.

**Key NPCs**: Zariel (archduchess, fallen angel, general)

---

### Demon Hordes (Blood War opponents)
**Type**: Chaotic evil armies | **Base**: The Abyss (invading Avernus)

Waves of chaotic demons crashing against Hell's lawful defenders. They have no organization, no strategy, and no shortage of bodies to throw at the problem. The Blood War is an eternal stalemate because demons are too chaotic to win and too numerous to lose.

**Novel role**: Environmental hazard. Demon attacks are storms -- unpredictable, violent, impersonal.

---

### The Wandering Emporium
**Type**: Traveling market | **Leader**: Mahadi (rakshasa)

A marketplace that appears wherever commerce is needed. Run by Mahadi, who considers himself a businessman and his customers as long-term investments. Every deal is fair. Every deal also has fine print. Mahadi never cheats anyone -- he simply makes deals that are technically fair but practically devastating.

**Novel role**: Comic relief and worldbuilding. The Emporium is Adams-level absurd: a shopping mall in Hell, complete with customer service, return policies, and the unshakeable feeling that you're being slowly eaten.

---

### Tiamat's Court
**Type**: Dragon goddess's entourage | **Base**: Avernus (imprisoned)

Tiamat, the five-headed dragon goddess, is technically imprisoned in Avernus. "Imprisoned" in this context means "nobody has the courage to tell her she can't leave." Her court of dragon-worshipping cultists and draconic servants maintains a presence throughout Avernus.

**Novel role**: Volume 3's ultimate reveal. The five colors, five shadows, five rings -- details planted across the arc converge into the revelation that Tiamat is involved. This is the Sanderson-style payoff: a surprise that feels inevitable in retrospect.

---

### Independent Powers

**Asmodeus**: CEO of Hell. Delegates everything. Mentioned but never seen -- his absence is more threatening than his presence would be.

**Gargauth (The Lord of Betrayers)**: Fallen archdevil sealed within Kalmaris. Central to Kalmaris's personal arc. See `03-magic-system.md` for full mechanics.

**Mad Maggie**: Night hag running Fort Knucklebone. Eccentric collector, unreliable ally, and the kind of person who calls you "dear" while contemplating your market value. Helps the party for reasons of her own.

**Brimstone Bill**: Traveling war machine mechanic. Appears when least expected and most needed. Charges accordingly.
