# Glossary

## Characters

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Milo Quickspark** | Gnome alchemist, terrible cook, excellent friend. Keeps a journal. |
| **Kalmaris** | Aarakocra bard, former pirate of the Emerald Crow, hosts an echo of Gargauth. Do NOT call him "Calamari." |
| **Lamil Morgenhauch** | Elf druid, Circle of Wildfire, leader of the Ember Brotherhood. Says more with silence than most people say with words. |
| **Lufnik Springveil** | Human soldier, moon-resurrected, secret water powers. The puddles are not coincidence. |
| **Longjohn Silver** | Human (air genasi heritage), former pirate of the Galeheart, beast master. Lost Blacky. Lost everything. Hasn't stopped. |
| **Serox Kieselstein** | Dwarf armorer from Zornstein, "the Iron Aesthete." Has never been wrong about anything, ever, according to him. |
| **Blobby** | Milo's alchemical familiar. Species: unclear. Diet: everything, especially Milo's cooking. Communication: wobbling. |
| **Blacky** | Longjohn's boar companion. Died fighting the magma elemental. His soul may be in Hell. |

## Places

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Moreva** | Tropical island where the party shipwrecked. Haunted by a shadow dragon. |
| **Zornstein** | Dwarven mountain kingdom. Excellent smiths. Terrible at admitting they have a heating problem. |
| **Baldur's Gate** | Port city on the Sword Coast. Population ~40,000. Runs on money, secrets, and mercenaries. |
| **Avernus** | First layer of the Nine Hells. Burning wasteland. Eternal war zone. No moon. No peace. Some excellent shopping. |
| **Hellturel / Elturel** | Holy city dragged into Avernus by infernal pact. Chained over the River Styx. |
| **Fort Knucklebone** | Mad Maggie's junkyard fortress. War machine garage and questionable hospitality. |
| **Bronze Citadel** | Zariel's headquarters. Part palace, part weapon, part bad day. |
| **Wandering Emporium** | Traveling marketplace. Fair deals, fine print, inevitable regret. |
| **Eldariel Forest** | Lamil's home. Ancient elf forest, seat of the Ember Brotherhood. |
| **Airspire** | Floating citadel. Longjohn's birthplace. |
| **Eldertree Glade** | Gnome village. Milo's birthplace. Smells faintly of explosions and bad cooking. |
| **River Styx** | Flows through all layers of Hell. Contact erases memories. Do not swim. |

## Organizations

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Flaming Fists** | Mercenary company / city guard of Baldur's Gate |
| **Azure-Enklave** | Mage guild. Secretive. Knowledge-hoarding. |
| **RR-Guild** | "Rebellische Racher" -- resistance movement. Good cause, questionable methods. |
| **Mechanists' Collective** | Milo's guild. Alchemists and tinkerers. Perpetually singed. |
| **Ember Brotherhood** | Lamil's druid circle. Fire as renewal, not destruction. |
| **Liberation Brigade** | Lufnik's former rebel army. |

## Magic Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Reagent** | Raw material for alchemy. Quality and purity affect results. |
| **Physical Toll** | Physical cost the alchemist pays for every creation (fatigue, nosebleeds, sensory loss) |
| **Soul Coin** | Damned soul forged into currency. Screams when used as fuel. |
| **Spent Coin** | Drained soul coin. Common currency in Avernus. |
| **Charged Coin** | Full-power soul coin. Valuable fuel. Aware soul inside. |
| **Named Coin** | Rare coin containing a notable soul. Often lucid. |
| **Obsidian Jeton** | Common currency of Avernus. Backed by soul coin reserves. |
| **Infernal War Machine** | Hell's preferred transport. Powered by soul coins or mortal vitality. Loud, fast, unreliable. |
| **Wildfire** | Lamil's magic. Cleansing fire that renews. Corrupted in Avernus. |
| **Gargauth** | The Lord of Betrayers. Fallen archdevil. Echo sealed in Kalmaris. |
| **The Vessel** | What devils call Kalmaris. He hates it almost as much as "Calamari." |
| **Moon Gift** | Lufnik's water control powers, granted by the moon during resurrection |
| **Blobulus** | Giant corrupted version of Blobby, created by the RR-Guild. Boss fight in Volume 2. |

## Supporting Characters

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Captain Zodge** | Flaming Fists captain. Pragmatic, mission-focused. Assigns the Avernus mission. |
| **Seraphina Sterling** | Milo's academic rival. Enchantress obsessed with order and precision. |
| **Peeblewitz Steinguckern** | Gnome tourist and insurance agent. Brief comic relief ally during V2 rescue. |
| **Kaelin Sturmreiter** | Azure-Enklave member. Killed during the auction attack. |
| **Garrick** | Iron Circle sharpshooter. Killed during the auction attack. |
| **Dr. Thallus** | Doctor in Widowhall who sealed Kalmaris's mirror demon. |
| **Mad Maggie** | Night hag running Fort Knucklebone. Eccentric, dangerous, "helpful." |
| **Mahadi** | Rakshasa running the Wandering Emporium. Customer service as predation. |
| **Zariel** | Archduchess of Avernus. Fallen angel. General of Hell's armies. |
| **Morgath Dunkelschatten** | Lamil's corrupted ex-friend. Uses perverted wildfire magic. |
| **Arian Brightwater** | Lufnik's missing best friend from childhood. May or may not be real. |

## Creatures

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Shadow Dragon** | The antagonist beneath Moreva. Feeds on sanity. |
| **Magma Elemental** | Stole a fire god's power. Final boss of Volume 1's mountain arc. Killed Blacky. |
| **Tarrasque** | City-sized monster. Mostly sleeps. When awake, the recommended response is "run." |
| **Lemure** | Lowest form of devil. Barely conscious. Made from processed damned souls. |

## In-World Documents

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **The Alchemist's Logbook** | Milo's journal. Excerpts appear as blue boxes throughout the novel. |
| **The Somewhat Unreliable Guide to [X]** | Milo's travel guide entries. Blue-boxed chapter openers in Volume 3. Adams-style worldbuilding. |
| **Alchemist's Notes** | Milo's scientific observations. Blue-boxed throughout. |

## Slang & Idioms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **"Thermally inadvisable"** | Milo-speak for "on fire" or "dangerously hot" |
| **"Suboptimal outcome"** | Milo-speak for "disaster" |
| **"The wind's changed"** | Longjohn-speak for "things are shifting" |
| **"Lee shore"** | Longjohn-speak for "dangerous position" |
| **"Structurally unsound"** | Serox-speak for "bad idea" or "unreliable person" |
| **"Load-bearing"** | Serox-speak for "important" or "reliable" |
| **"Obviously"** | Serox-speak for "I am about to say something that is not at all obvious" |
